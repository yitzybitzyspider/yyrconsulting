Recent engagement

Rebuilding operations for a multimillion-dollar equipment marketplace

An equipment marketplace was triple-counting millions of dollars of inventory across spreadsheets and a half-implemented ERP. Wrong units were shipping, test results weren't attached to the gear they described, and leadership had no reliable view of what was actually on the shelf.

In a matter of weeks, YYR designed and shipped a custom operations platform covering serial-level tracking, receiving and testing procedures, product photography workflow, cycle counts, and leadership dashboards, integrated with the company's ERP and CRM, then trained the operations team to run it independently.

  • Errors near zero: shipping and inventory mistakes engineered out of the process
  • One source of truth: the whole warehouse tracked unit-by-unit in one platform
  • Cash recovered: a seven-figure recovery pipeline built from idle and forgotten stock
  • Accounting unblocked: the ERP finally running the business end to end
  • Real handoff: a non-technical operations team runs everything day-to-day
