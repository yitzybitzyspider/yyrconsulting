Recent engagement

Rebuilding operations for a $15M equipment marketplace

A professional AV equipment marketplace was triple-counting roughly $10M of inventory across spreadsheets and a half-implemented ERP. Wrong serial numbers were shipping, test results weren't attached to units, and leadership had no reliable view of what was on the shelf.

In eight weeks, YYR designed and shipped a custom operations platform — serial-level tracking, receiving and testing procedures, 360° product photography workflow, cycle counts, and KPI dashboards — integrated with the company's ERP and CRM, then trained the operations team to run it independently.

  • Errors near zero: inventory discrepancies fell from 100+ per area to 3–4
  • One source of truth: $10M of inventory tracked serial-by-serial in one platform
  • ~$1M recovered: a disposition and asset-recovery process for idle stock
  • Accounting unblocked: ERP fully operationalized, warehouse wired to the books
  • Real handoff: a non-technical operations team runs everything day-to-day
