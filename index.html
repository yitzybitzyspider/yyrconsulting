Rebuilding operations for a multimillion-dollar equipment marketplace.

The company was triple-counting millions of dollars of inventory across spreadsheets and a half-implemented ERP. Wrong units were shipping, test results were not attached to the gear they described, and leadership had no reliable view of what was on the shelf.

In a matter of weeks, YYR designed and shipped a custom operations platform: serial-level tracking, receiving and testing procedures, product photography, cycle counts and leadership dashboards, connected to the ERP and CRM. Then the operations team was trained to run it on their own.

  • Errors near zero. Shipping and inventory mistakes engineered out of the process.
  • One source of truth. The whole warehouse tracked unit by unit in one platform.
  • Cash recovered. A seven-figure recovery pipeline built from idle stock.
  • Accounting unblocked. The ERP running the business end to end.
  • A real handoff. A non-technical team runs it day to day.

Diagnose, design, build, hand off.

  1. Diagnose

    Structured interviews across every department the process touches. The gap between how work should flow and how it does is where the money is.

  2. Design

    No off-the-shelf template. Each system is reasoned from what the business needs to measure and move, so teams stop working around their tools.

  3. Build

    AI-assisted development turns a six-month software project into weeks, so the budget goes into a working system instead of a pilot.

  4. Hand off

    The job is done when your team, technical or not, runs the system without me. Documentation and training come with every engagement.
